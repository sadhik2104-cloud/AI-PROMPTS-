The useful difference

Structure that gets you past the blank page.

A prompt should do more than sound clever. The playbook helps you give an AI system enough direction to create a draft you can actually review and adapt.

  1. 01
    Set the job

    Give the model a role and a concrete task.

  2. 02
    Add useful context

    Supply audience, source material, goals, and boundaries.

  3. 03
    Shape the output

    Ask for the format and level of detail you can use.

  4. 04
    Check before acting

    Make assumptions visible, verify claims, and choose next steps.

AI can be wrong. The prompts encourage careful review; they do not replace professional judgment.

COPY-READY PROMPT PREVIEW

Act as a marketing planner for a small [type of business]. Using the offer, audience, budget range, and capacity below, draft a four-week test plan. Include one objective, three channel experiments, weekly actions, a simple measurement method, and risks. Separate facts from assumptions; leave unknown figures blank. Ask up to three essential questions, or state assumptions and proceed.

A few useful details

Good questions,
straight answers.

Do I need a specific AI tool?

No. The prompts are written as adaptable instructions rather than being tied to one named AI product.

Will these prompts guarantee a correct answer?

No prompt can guarantee that. The playbook encourages you to provide source material, make assumptions visible, and verify important claims before relying on them.

Can I customize the prompts?

Yes. Replace the bracketed fields with your audience, goal, permitted context, constraints, tone, and preferred output format.

Who is this eBook for?

Business owners, creators, freelancers, teams, learners, and anyone looking for practical starting points for common work and creative tasks.
